# Locator source and scope

The film cuts from actual recorded Minecraft scenery to a separate Singapore map. Only the 256 x 256 metre Market Street CBD district is generated. The map does not depict playable coverage of the whole island.

The main-island outline comes from Natural Earth Admin 0 v5.1.1, scale 1:10 million, public domain. Made with Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/. The generalized outline does not separately depict offshore islands or Pedra Branca.

The marker is the centre of the supplied district bounds, latitude 1.283, longitude 103.85. It is not a surveyed court location. Exact bounds, source hashes and projection are retained in locator-film-v3-provenance.json. Facade resemblance and final release acceptance remain separate checks.
